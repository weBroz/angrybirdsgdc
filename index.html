HOW TO PLAY

🎯

Slingshot Aim & Launch

Drag the bird back from the slingshot, adjust power and angle using the trajectory dots, and release to fling!

🔴

Red Bird

Solid balanced impact, reliable against wood structures and direct pig hits.

🟡

Chuck (Yellow Bird)

Tap/Click while flying to trigger high-speed rocket boost! Pierces wood easily.

🔵

The Blues (Blue Bird)

Tap/Click while flying to split into 3 birds! Destroys glass and ice instantly.

💣

Bomb (Black Bird)

Tap/Click to detonate in a massive explosion! Demolishes stone and heavily pushes blocks.

⚪

Matilda (White Bird)

Tap/Click to drop an explosive egg bomb while rocketing upward!

🧨

TNT Explosive Crates

Hit TNT boxes with birds or debris to trigger chain reaction blast waves!
